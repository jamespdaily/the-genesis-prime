# Quests

- **Pick the quest from the game tables before asking whether the PC is free.** Find a giver spawn whose Creature2 `questIdGiven` lists a quest the character meets, whose objectives are all types this server counts. Give the character the prior quests a quest needs as `completed` rows in `character.quest`, while the server is stopped. "Huge Success!" (5628) has a talk objective and a kill objective: it needs 5626 and 5627, its giver is Supervisor Renla (spawn 61118, (-4750.3, -915.4, -1279.8)), and it is turned in to Minion Togor (spawn 61117, (-4847.5, -940.5, -1202.3)).
- **Pick a quest whose giver the development character's faction can reach.** Gob Stopper is Dominion. A giver's spawn `faction1` of 170 is Dominion; 171 is Exile, and its guards kill the character on entering, as at Commander Durek's camp in world 426 (quest 3741).
- **Check that the character can see the quest's units.** A unit whose Creature2 `prerequisiteIdAnimState` fails for the character is hidden and offers no prompt. Quest 3771's Chua Datacenters (13096) need BaseFaction 167, Exile, and the development character is 166, Dominion (`character.character.faction_id`). The fact file has the client's addresses for it.
- **Check whether the objectives are sequential before giving a quest by database.** Quest 3886's huts stay hidden until objective 0, a torch, is done. Setting that objective's progress in the database left the tracker with no open line, and no prompt showed. Take such a quest from its giver and play each objective in order.
- **Stand beside an object, not at its spawn point.** At a hut's spawn point the camera sat inside the hut's model, and nothing could be seen.
- **Summon a creature that an objective names but the world has no spawn of.** "Huge Success!"'s talk objective names Minion Togor 24687 and its kill names Experiment 878 (24697). Both work summoned. A summoned creature appears on the player, so step back (`playtest key 0x1F 1200`) before its prompt shows.
- **A summoned receiver takes a turn-in.** When the quest's receiver has no spawn near the character, `!entity summon` it and talk to it.
- **Test collect objectives with GM `!item add`.** "Gather the Goods" (4645) has three: items 12406 ×5, 12407 ×5 and 12408 ×3, turned in to 17035. Add the items in steps: the tracker shows a percent, as each count is 2 or more. Delete one to see the quest go back to accepted, then add it again and turn the quest in.
- **Test collectables with "Better Than This" (6581).** Priestess Trania Lavus (spawn 65318, world 22, (-3173.3, -792.5, -3689.9)) gives it; it needs 8 herbs from four collectable creatures, the nearest spawn 65447 at (-3238.1, -800.9, -3629.3). The units show only once the quest is taken. Stand at (-3236.6, -800.5, -3629.3) and step back (`playtest key 0x1F 400`) until the prompt reads "Collect". A collected unit hides from its collector for 60 s and then returns, without a move; logging in again shows it at once, which collects all 8 in about 3 minutes. Summon her (`!entity summon 30610`) to turn in.
- **F (`0x21`) talks to or activates the unit whose prompt shows.** A quest giver's dialog lists its quests; click one to read it, then click the accept line.
- **The accept dialog previews the rewards** at the top left: XP, reputation, credits and the item choice. Compare the turn-in's chat lines and the credits at the bottom right against it.
- **L (`0x26`) opens the quest log.** Its Finished filter is at `playtest click 372 1629`. The log and the tracker hide some objectives, such as the SucceedCSI ones under "Cause and Defect"'s checklist; read their progress from `character.quest_objective` after logging out.
- **Change the character's place by logging out and editing its row**, as no command teleports. The login after doubles as the check that the quest log is kept.
