# Finding a fight

- **Enter where one creature attacks alone.** Near natural spawns, several creatures attack at once, ranged ones out of Shred's reach, and they kill the player. Before entering, set the dev character's saved place to the recording's spot: `update character.character set location_x = -5278.26, location_y = -944.742, location_z = -1272.08, rotation_x = 0.52 where id = 1` in `genesis_prime_dev`. There only Grund (unit 96) is within the 15 m aggro range, and it attacks on sight, as in the recording.
- **To fight a summoned creature alone there, die and wake first.** Grund joins any fight at the recording's spot. After Wake Here, the creatures in range stay calm, so a creature summoned then fights alone.
- **Check that the step can be met in the client before asking whether the PC is free.** A scripted creature, for one, can be reachable only by summoning it with its script's name.
- **Play a spell's whole chain in a test before a live login of it.** Check that nothing in it is refused and that it holds an effect that shows, such as Damage. A spell whose only effect has no handler lands and shows nothing: a live login of Transference (effect type 5) showed no damage.
- **Put an ability on the bar while the server is stopped.** Add its Spell4Base to `character.spell` and a row to `character.action_set_shortcut` (shortcut type 4, tier 1) in `genesis_prime_dev`. Locations 3 and 4 are slots 4 and 5, pressed with scan codes `0x05` and `0x06`. Delete both rows afterwards. `character.spell` takes a Spell4Base id, not a Spell4 id. Only a Spell4Base of type 5, a class ability, shows on the bar. Spell4Base 41477 is a type 38 row and stayed off it. The Engineer's spell 41477 is Spell4 41477, whose base is 25673.
- **Give the character the vital an ability costs.** The development character is a Stalker and has no focus row in `character.stat`, so another class's spell that costs focus is refused with cast result 5, `SpellBad`. Insert stat 1 (focus) for the login, and delete it afterwards.
- **Focus inserted that way lasts one cast.** The server holds focus to the focus pool, and a Stalker's pool is 0, so the first cast's cost leaves 0 focus and every later press shows "Spell data is bad". Plan one cast per login, and set the focus row again while the server is stopped before the next.
- **To check a heal on other players, cast Spellslinger's Sustain (43326, base 27504) among the walkers.** It heals through its proxy, 43383, everyone in a 20-unit circle, with no cast time, so it reaches the walkers on every cast; it healed 4 of 8, its target limit. A telegraph heal such as Healing Torrent (39116) has a rectangle a few units long, which a walker crossed less than one cast in five.
- **To check a buff on other players, cast the Engineer's 41756 (base 25951) among the walkers.** It adds 0.08 to properties 112 and 164 for everyone in a 25-unit circle for 10 s, with no target needed. Read the change from the walkers' recordings, as the next point says.
- **To see what another player took, read that player's recording.** The world records every connection's packets, the walkers' too, in `~/Coding/wildstar/eldan-recordings`, so a walker's file holds every message it was sent, with its time. The walkers' screens cannot be read, and their health bars hide an overheal.
- **To check a heal, read its green number over the player, not the health.** Out of combat the player regenerates fast: from 3,000 health to 9,223 in about 20 seconds after entering, so the gain mixes the heal and regeneration. Shoot within a second of the cast ending; the number fades.
- **Summon the creature instead of walking to one.** `playtest chat "!entity summon <creature id>"` builds that creature from its Creature2 row where the player stands, in any world, and a hostile one attacks on sight. 9633 is a Savannah Poacher and 9634 is the recording's creature; the client names it Savannah Marksman, its Creature2 name. Steering by screenshot to reach a creature took six rounds of keys and screenshots.
- **To see a state on a creature, fight the Savannah Marksman, 9634.** Stagger's damage killed a poacher (456 health) before its stun showed; on the Marksman, a champion, stun stars circled its head for the stun's 2.5 seconds. A summoned creature stands on the player, so step back (`playtest key 0x1F 700`) before a cone.
- **A forced move of the player is the client's own.** It moves its player itself when the server leaves it movement control (0x1405d6c10), so a live login checks that the player lands where the move takes it and stays there, with no snap back a second later.
- **Name a script after the creature id to run it.** `!entity summon 71074 RavenousReaperEntityScript` brings a Reaper that casts its scripted spells. The map refuses an unknown script name in chat. Without a name, a creature runs no script, as every spawn here does.
- **Tab picks the nearest hostile or neutral unit, never a walker.** A yellow name is neutral, and Shred does not hurt it; a red name is hostile. Check the target frame after each Tab. The client has no `/target` command.
- **Shred strikes a cone in front of the player.** Face the target first: holding A (`0x1E`) turns the player left.
- **Press Shred about once a second.** Faster presses land in the global cooldown.
- **To test presses during the global cooldown, switch off hold to continue casting.** With it on, each press is `ClientCastSpellContinuous` and every early press reaches the server; with it off, presses are `ClientCastSpell`, which the client queues in the last 500 ms. Close the client, back up `%APPDATA%\NCSOFT\WildStar\User.ini` on the PC, add `spell.holdToContinueCasting=false`, and restore the backup after the login. `ClientCombatOptions` then carries casting options 3, not 11.
- **Bar slot 3 holds 23173 (Spell4 38791), pressed with `0x04`.** Its global cooldown is enum 1, 750 ms, against Shred's 1000 ms, so a pair of the two shows both lengths.
- Keys go by scan code: Tab `0x0F`, 1 (Shred on the recorded character's bar) `0x02`, W `0x11`, A `0x1E`, Escape `0x01`. Escape with nothing targeted opens the options menu; press it again to close it.
- **A dash needs two taps closer together than `playtest key` sends them.** Each `playtest key` is its own SSH call, and two calls in a row did not dash.
- **The client sends the pressed sprint only in combat.** Out of combat the out-of-combat sprint, 80529, already runs, and Shift (`playtest down 0x2A`, with W held) sends no `ClientSetSprintState`. Summon a poacher (9633) first; in combat, Shift sends it, and its release sends sprint off.
- **To die, summon about ten creatures and wait.** Ten Grunds kill the player in under 40 seconds. The "You Are Dead" dialog's Wake Here button is at `playtest click 1917 354`.
