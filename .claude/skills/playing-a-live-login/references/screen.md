# Reading the screen

- **Time a window of a few seconds with a `playtest shot` loop.** Shots in a loop come about 0.7 s apart. A `playtest chat` command takes about 3 s to type, so it cannot time a reading inside such a window.
- **To time presses and shots closer than 0.7 s, run them in one `playtest do`.** Press with `[EldanScreen]::Key(0x02, 30)`, wait with `Start-Sleep -Milliseconds`, time with a `[Diagnostics.Stopwatch]`, and shoot with `[EldanScreen]::Shot('C:\eldan-playtest\s1.jpg')` in a loop, about 110 ms apart. Copy the shots back with `scp`, and crop the action bar with `sips -c 140 400 --cropOffset 1990 1490`.
- **A change in the player's health is not a damage number.** The player's health regenerates between hits, so the difference between two readings mixes damage and regeneration.
- Damage the player deals shows in white over the target. Damage the player takes shows in red beside it, and a creature's crit on it showed in orange.
- "You cannot do that yet" over the player means the press came during the global cooldown.
- **The client never shows chat sent while it loads.** The server's "New Zone: (585)..." line goes out before the client sends `ClientEnteredWorld`, so it never appears. Read the zone from `ClientEnteredWorld(zone)` in a client message log, or from the saved `world_zone_id`.
- **`!entity properties` prints about 170 lines, more than the chat keeps.** Its header, which names the unit, scrolls out of the history, so a property report cannot show whose it is. Scroll the chat with `[EldanMouse]::Wheel(300, 1800, n)`, about one line a notch, positive up; the chat stays where it was scrolled. The reports of a dead creature and the player can both look plausible.
- **To follow a property or a buff over time, read the recording instead.** The world records every message it sends, with its time, in the newest `eldan.world-*` file in `~/Coding/wildstar/eldan-recordings`. Read the `ServerEntityPropertiesUpdate`, `ServerSpellStart` and `ServerSpellBuffRemove` lines. Screenshots could not show an aura's buff refreshed every 500 ms; temporary logging of the same messages did, before the world recorded them.
- **To check where a creature walks, read its `ServerEntityCommand` lines in the recording too.** Screenshots of a chase were blocked by trees. Each walk is a `SetPositionPath` command with its points. Log on the server only what no message carries, such as the point a creature sighted the player from, and remove the logging before committing.
